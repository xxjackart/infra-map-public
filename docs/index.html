02 / Engineering patterns

Engineering patterns behind the map.

The important part is not the logo collection. It is the set of constraints that keeps the system understandable, portable and operable.

01

Local-first. External by exception.

Systems of record and daily workflows remain under direct control. External models and utilities are replaceable interfaces, not the foundation.

Self-hostedDockerOpen data
02

Agentic, but bounded.

Agents use explicit tools with narrow scopes. Read, recommend and execute are different permission levels, with approval matched to impact.

MCPLeast privilegeHITL
03

Knowledge outlives the model.

Durable documents, structured records and curated retrieval stay useful when models change. RAG adds context, not another source of truth.

RAGRedmineMarkdown
04

Failure domains stay separate.

Local automation, DNS and independent uptime checks continue when another operating domain is unavailable. Resilience is functional, not cosmetic.

Multi-siteDNSIsolation
05

Operations must be visible.

Metrics, logs and uptime checks cover selected services and pipelines. Dashboards support diagnosis; alerts trigger an operational response.

GrafanaLokiUptime Kuma
06

Recovery has explicit rules.

Operating standard: sensitive backups must be encrypted before off-site transfer. Recovery procedures and restore tests are part of the same standard.

PolicyEncryptionRestore tests

04 / Operating principles

Operating principles for control and recovery.

Good infrastructure is not the part that impresses during a demo. It is the part that remains understandable during change and failure.

  1. 01

    Data before models.

    Models are interchangeable. Knowledge, tools and policies remain stable.

  2. 02

    Tools before guesses.

    Agents read authoritative state through APIs instead of relying on assumed context.

  3. 03

    Approval before impact.

    Automation level follows scope, reversibility and consequence.

  4. 04

    Failures must be visible.

    Monitoring and recovery are designed together with the service.

  5. 05

    Public documentation is scoped by a threat model.

    Share the architecture and the reasoning. Keep the targets private.
